# FAQ

## Does Castix require Shizuku?

No. Shizuku is optional.

## Does Castix require root?

No. Root is optional.

## Does Castix bundle Shizuku?

It does not bundle Shizuku as a mandatory external environment. The current build does include Shizuku API/provider dependencies for integration.

## Does Castix bundle LSPosed?

No. The LSPosed runtime is external and optional.

## Does Castix bundle LSPatch?

No. LSPatch is external and optional.

## Is Dhizuku required?

No. Dhizuku is optional.

## What Android versions are supported?

Android 7.0 / API 24 and newer.

## Is Castix open source?

Yes.

## Does Castix use Firebase?

The current build does not include a Firebase dependency.

## Does Castix use Google Play Services?

The current build does not include a Google Play Services dependency.

## Does Castix require an online account?

Core Castix functionality does not require an online account.

## Does Castix collect zero data?

This Wiki does not make an absolute zero-data guarantee. Castix is designed as a local Android utility; implementation-specific behavior should be verified against the current source/release.

## Why does behavior differ between devices?

Android version, manufacturer, ROM, battery policies, target-app behavior, backend environment, and permissions can affect results.

## Where do I report bugs?

https://github.com/elhizazi1/Castix/issues
