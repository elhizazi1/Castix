Privacy

Castix is designed as a local Android utility. This page describes the project's privacy approach at a high level.

No account required

Castix does not require an online account to use its core functionality.

No bundled analytics

The project does not bundle third-party analytics or advertising SDKs as part of the application.

Optional Android backends

Features using Shizuku, Dhizuku, Root, LSPosed, LSPatch, or Accessibility depend on the corresponding Android environment and permissions configured by the user.

Open source

You can inspect the source code and project changes on GitHub.
