About Castix

Castix is an open-source Android utility focused on managing background playback and related playback restrictions through several privilege and accessibility backends.

What makes Castix different?

Castix brings several Android environments together in one interface. Depending on the device and configuration, users can work with Accessibility, Shizuku, Dhizuku, Root, LSPosed, or LSPatch.
