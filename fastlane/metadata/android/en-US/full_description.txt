Castix is an Android utility for managing background playback and related playback restrictions through several privilege and accessibility backends.

Features:
• Shizuku, Dhizuku, Root, LSPosed, LSPatch, and Android Accessibility backends.
• Backend status and permission checks from the main interface.
• Target-app selection for supported playback workflows.
• AMOLED / black-screen mode with configurable clock styles.
• Adjustable clock scale, vertical position, and spacing.
• Quick Settings support for black-screen mode.
• Multilingual interface, including Arabic, English, Chinese, Japanese, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Italian, Russian, and Turkish.

Castix does not bundle Shizuku, Dhizuku, LSPosed, LSPatch, or a root solution. Features that use these backends require the corresponding service or environment to be installed and configured on the device.
